How it works
Whenever you have too many tabs, click the OneTab icon to convert them into a list. When you need them again, you can restore them one by one or all at once.

When your tabs are in the OneTab list, you can save up to 95% of memory because fewer tabs are open in Google Chrome.

Privacy assurance
OneTab is designed for privacy. Your tab URLs are never transmitted or disclosed to the OneTab developers or any other party. Icons for tab URL domains are generated by Google. The only exception is if you intentionally use the 'Share as a web page' feature, which uploads your tab list to a web page so you can share it with others. Tabs are never shared unless you specifically use the 'Share as a web page' button.

Additional Benefits
Storing tabs in OneTab can also speed up your computer by reducing your browser's CPU load and memory (RAM) usage. Even a high-end computer can become sluggish when many browser windows are open, because each open window may constantly consume resources.

More Features
Easily export and import lists of tabs, or share your list as a web page with other people or devices. Get organized using folders, tasks, notes and encrypted cross-browser sync.

You will not lose your tab list when you close OneTab, restart your browser or computer, or if your browser crashes.
